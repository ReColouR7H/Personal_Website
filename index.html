About me

I am Andrés Soto, a Peruvian economist trained at Universidad del Pacífico. I use datasets and national surveys to study public policy questions, with a particular interest in water and sanitation services.

My experience includes tariff model design and public investment portfolio appraisal. My research on rural water systems examines chlorination practices and the institutional conditions that influence service continuity.

I provide analytical support for consulting engagements and applied research. I document my methods and prepare findings that teams can review and use in their decisions.
